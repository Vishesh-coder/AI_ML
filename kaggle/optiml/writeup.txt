1. DATA JOINS, TIMESTAMPS/CLOCKS, AND MISSING VALUES
----------------------------------------------------
- Data Joins:
  Customer demographics were integrated using a point-in-time backward merge_asof
  join on record_updated_at against order_ts keyed on customer_id. This eliminated
  future data leakage from 2025 profile updates into earlier orders. A fallback to
  the customer's earliest recorded record was applied for orders preceding the first
  update timestamp. Store and product attributes were joined via standard relational
  left joins on store_id and product_id. Twenty anomalous records from December 2022
  prior to platform launch were removed. Twelve product category typographical
  errors were normalized into 32 canonical groups.

- Timestamps and Clocks:
  Temporal lifecycles were constructed by calculating elapsed days for customer
  tenure (order_ts - signup_date), product market age (order_ts - launch_date), and
  store operating age (order_ts - opened_date). Cyclic temporal signals were
  captured via order hour, day of the week, and year-month cohorts. Behavioral
  sequence intervals were modeled by extracting inter-order elapsed durations at both
  the customer-category level and the customer-order level.

- Missing Value Treatment:
  Physical product dimensions (length, width, height) and weight were imputed using
  domain medians to derive volume, density, and surface area. Missing values in
  discounts and freight charges were imputed to zero with explicit missingness
  indicators (discount_is_na, freight_is_na). Missing categorical entries were
  consistently assigned a dedicated "missing" category for native tree handling.


2. VALIDATION METHODOLOGY AND EXACT SPLIT
-----------------------------------------
- A strict chronological Out-of-Time (OOT) validation framework was established to
  mirror the test horizon and eliminate temporal lookahead bias.
- The 24-month training set (January 2023 to December 2024) was partitioned into
  four 6-month temporal epochs:
    * Slice 1: Jan 2023 - Jun 2023
    * Slice 2: Jul 2023 - Dec 2023
    * Slice 3: Jan 2024 - Jun 2024
    * Slice 4: Jul 2024 - Dec 2024 (Holdout Validation Split: 351,923 rows)
- To avoid target leakage in stacking, Empirical Bayes log-odds were learned on
  Slices 1 and 2 to transform Slice 3. The meta-stacker was trained on Slice 3 and
  validated unconditionally on holdout Slice 4.


3. MODEL ARCHITECTURE AND SCORE-IMPROVING FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------
- Architecture:
  A Decoupled Two-Tower architecture was deployed:
  * Tower 1 (Structural LightGBM): Trained with native categorical handling on
    domain-engineered features including route corridors (cust_region -> store_region),
    intra-region fulfillment friction, product volume and density, line spend
    share, customer sequence timing, and basket-level bracketing signals (e.g.,
    duplicate category purchases within an order, capturing size-bracketing returns).
  * Tower 2 (Empirical Bayes Stacker): L2-regularized Logistic Regression trained on
    out-of-time target log-odds of high-cardinality interaction entities
    (store_x_region, route_x_format, supplier_code, and product_id).

- Score Improvements & Post-Processing:
  * Combining Tower 1 and Tower 2 in log-odds space improved validation AUC by
    +0.00237 over the baseline LightGBM.
  * Within-Order Peer Smoothing: For multi-item orders, individual item probabilities
    were shrunk toward the order mean (alpha = 0.20), reflecting basket-level
    correlated return propensity.
  * Monthly Percentile Rank Alignment: Predictions were blended 50% global percentile
    rank and 50% within-month percentile rank, preserving local ordering while
    neutralizing macroeconomic cart-size shifts in the test period.
  * Final inference utilized a 3-seed full-dataset ensemble refit (seeds 42, 2026, 777).
